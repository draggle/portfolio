In my [post](/writing/1) earlier this year, I wanted to come back to SF this summer. Though I didn't get to come for an internship (see [here](https://lnkd.in/p/gVRw3Aqd) D: ), I spent a week in SF to attend [Y Combinator's Startup School](https://events.ycombinator.com/startup-school-2026)! It was cool to meet and be surrounded by so many interesting people building great things and getting to hear from people building at the frontier (Jensen Huang, Sam Altman, Boris Cherny, Alexandr Wang, and Peter Steinberger to name a few)!
